We are facing multiple related issues in our hours management system.

Issue 1 – Dashboard not updating in real time:
When users add or withdraw hours, the dashboard does not consistently reflect the changes automatically. In some cases, a manual browser refresh (CTRL + R) is required. This behavior is inconsistent and confusing for users.

Issue 2 – Missing approval history for approvers:
After an approver approves or denies pending requests within their department, they can no longer view the approval/denial history. The records appear to disappear entirely, making it impossible to audit past decisions.

Task:
Analyze these issues, identify likely root causes (state management, backend validation, caching, or data lifecycle problems), and propose clear, ensure real-time UI updates, and preserve approval history visibility for approvers.
